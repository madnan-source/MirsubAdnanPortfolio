Unreal Engine · Virtual reality

VR Climate Change: Lahore Smog

An interactive VR experience showing how smog and air pollution build up over time in Lahore. The user walks along a tree-lined city road as the air slowly fills with haze until the street almost disappears.

My role covered environment design, 3D assets, lighting and sound. Interaction and scripting were completed collaboratively with my team.

  • Environment design
  • Lighting
  • Sound

Maya · Arnold

Post-Apocalyptic City

A ruined city environment of collapsing high-rises, a broken elevated road and piles of rubble on a dusty landscape.

I focused on modular building pieces, realistic weathered textures and moody lighting to tell the story of a city left behind.
