About me

From communication to creative technology.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Communications and New Media Technology from Beaconhouse National University and am currently studying for an MSc in Interactive Digital Media at Trinity College Dublin.

My experience covers digital marketing, social media strategy, UGC, visual storytelling, 3D visualisation and interactive media. I have lived and studied in Oman, Pakistan and Ireland, which shapes how I think about audiences and culture.

Outside of study and client work I run Streets & Fits, my menswear and lifestyle page.
